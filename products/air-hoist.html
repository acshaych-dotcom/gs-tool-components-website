Air Hoist or Electric Hoist?

Choose an air hoist where electrical equipment is a risk or is not allowed — areas with flammable vapours, paint and solvent shops, or wet and wash-down zones. Air hoists also handle frequent start-stop lifting well.

Where none of these apply, an electric chain hoist is usually simpler to run. For weightless positioning of parts and tools, look at air balancers.

Air Hoist FAQs

What is an air hoist?

An air hoist (pneumatic hoist) lifts loads using compressed air instead of an electric motor. With no electrical spark source, it is used in paint shops, areas with flammable vapours and wash-down conditions.

What do I need to run an air hoist?

A compressed air supply at the lifting point. Share your load, lifting height and available air supply and we will suggest a suitable model.

Can an air hoist be fitted to a jib crane or rail system?

Yes. We mount air hoists on the same monorail / X-Y rail, jib crane and EOT structures we build.
