Electric Chain Hoist FAQs

What capacities of electric chain hoist do you supply?

From 125 Kg up to 30 Ton, with hook, eye or motor-driven trolley suspension.

What power supply does it need?

Three-phase AC, 380–415V, 50Hz. Controls run at a low 24V / 48V for operator safety.

What safety features are included?

A slipping clutch against overload, an electromagnetic DC disc brake that engages automatically on power failure, upper and lower limit switches, and an IP65 pendant with emergency stop.

Can it be fitted on my existing crane or rail?

Yes. Our chain hoists are mounted on X-Y rail and monorail systems, jib cranes and EOT cranes — including structures already in your plant.

Chain hoist or wire rope hoist?

Chain hoists are compact and economical for most workshop loads. For heavier loads, higher lifts or continuous duty, a wire rope hoist is usually the better fit.
